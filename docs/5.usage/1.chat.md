The chat is a panel docked beside the page. It stays open as you move between pages and after a reload. To open or close it, click the sparkle **Assistant** button in the dashboard header, or press `Ctrl` `Shift` `K` (`⌘` `⇧` `K` on macOS). To resize it, drag its edge.

![The chat panel open beside the Helio dashboard, with its starter prompts, Safe mode and Ask first](../images/captures/ai-chat-empty.webp)

If the header has no sparkle button, see [Installation](/docs/modules/assistant/installation).

## Ask for a change

1. Open the page you want to change. The composer shows its path in a chip and sends the page with your message. To leave the page out, close the chip.
2. Type your request, or click one of the three starters a new conversation offers for the page on screen. A starter marked **read only** changes nothing.
3. Press `Enter`. `Shift` `Enter` adds a line.

Three controls under the text field apply to this conversation only:

- The scope, **Safe mode** or **Code mode**. Safe mode changes the project only through the builder tools. Code mode may also edit any file and run commands.
- The approval mode, **Auto review** (Claude Code only), **Ask first**, **Accept edits** or **Plan only**. [Approval modes](/docs/modules/assistant/usage/settings#approval-modes) says what each one asks.
- The attachment button, for a mockup image or a screenshot. You can also drop files on the composer, up to 10 files of 25 MB each.

To find an earlier conversation, press `Ctrl` `J` (`⌘` `J` on macOS) or click the history button in the panel header. `/` focuses the search of the list. The header also has **New conversation**, and a **More** menu with **Open changes**, **Open activity**, **Settings** and the rules allowed in this chat.

## Follow a turn

While the assistant works, its tool calls show in groups such as **Used 6 tools**, with the actions they took and the time they took. Each row shows one call and its state: running, done, waiting for you, denied, blocked by safe mode, failed or stopped.

- To queue your next message, type it and press `Enter`. It runs after the turn.
- To stop the turn, press `Esc`.
- When the assistant edits a page, it can open that page in the dashboard, so you see the page reload as it changes.
- When the assistant asks a question, a card shows the suggested answers, **Other…** for your own words, and **Skip, you decide**. Pick an answer, then click **Next** or **Send answers**.
- In **Plan only**, the turn ends on a **Proposed plan** card that reads **Nothing has changed yet.** Click **Run with Ask first** to carry the plan out, or **Edit plan** to change it.

## Review what a turn changed

A turn that changed the project ends on a change set card. The card reads **Applied · checkpoint 1**, then the counts, such as **3 files changed · 3 Builder operations**. It shows the typecheck result (**Typecheck**, **Typecheck failed** or **No typecheck**) and each file with its added and removed lines.

- To read the diff, click **Review diff**.
- To restore the files as they were before the turn, click **Undo**. If later change sets touched the same files, a dialog titled **Later change sets touch the same files** lists them. Choose **Undo them together** or **Undo this one only**.
- After an undo, the card reads **Undone · checkpoint 1 restored** and offers **Redo**.

**May include changes from another chat** marks a set made while another conversation also changed files. When its own edit breaks the build, the assistant starts an auto-fix turn before it hands back, at most twice. A note reads **Auto-fix turn 1 of 2.** and lists the errors. To stop it, click **Stop auto-fix**. The change set of such a turn carries an **Auto-fix** tag.

The [Changes](/docs/modules/assistant/usage/changes) page lists every change set.

## Answer a request

When the assistant needs your approval, the panel shows the request under **Needs your approval · 1 of 1**, with a countdown such as **denies in 4:59**. The card names the action, such as **Run** and the command, and its effect, such as **adds a dependency**.

1. To see the diff, the full command or the address, click **Review**.
2. To allow more than this one request, pick a rule under **Allow**, such as **Every edit to this file**. **Only this edit**, **Only this command** and **Only this request** allow this request alone.
3. Click **Allow**, or press `Enter`.

To refuse, click **Deny** or press `N`. To refuse and tell the assistant what to do instead, click **Suggest a change**, type the instruction, then click **Deny and send**.

With several requests waiting, `J` and `K` move between them, and **Deny all and stop** refuses them all. A rule lasts until the chat ends or the assistant restarts. To revoke one, select it in the **More** menu. Requests for dependency files, git hook files and project configuration files never offer a rule.

If nobody answers before the countdown ends, the request is denied and the assistant is told why. **Ask again** sends the request back. When a request arrives while the panel is closed, a toast names it, with **Review** and **Later**. The toast needs **Notify me in the DMS**, which is on by default.

[Approval modes](/docs/modules/assistant/usage/settings#approval-modes) lists what asks in each mode.

### Confirm a deletion

The `dms` MCP server holds some builder calls until you confirm them: deleting pages, tables, fields, categories or queries, a database mapping that would remove a page, and a change of `antelope.config.ts`. It refuses the first call, which the steps show as **held until you confirm**. The agent repeats the call, and the chat asks you on a card first, in every mode.

- For a configuration change or a mapping, the card shows what the DMS previewed, such as the diff of `antelope.config.ts` or the pages the mapping removes.
- For a deletion, the card lists the call's arguments. Click **Delete** to run it. When deleting a table also clears its data, the card shows **Data loss**, and **Delete** stays off until you type the table's name. Undo restores the code, not the data.

## Ask from the command palette

Open the command palette with `Ctrl` `K` (`⌘` `K` on macOS). Its **Assistant** group has **Open the assistant**, **New conversation**, **Review approvals** and **Open changes**.

To ask a question without the panel, press `Tab` in the palette, then type the question. The answer is read-only and labelled **Read-only answer · Plan only, Safe mode**. To go on with it, click **Continue in the assistant**, which moves the conversation to the panel.

## Block previews

To compare layouts before the assistant changes a page, ask for them, for example "Show two layouts for this page before changing it". You can attach a mockup image to compare with.

The assistant answers with a **Block preview** card, which shows a thumbnail and a line such as **3 blocks · 2 variants · vs your mockup**.

1. Click **Preview** on the card. A dialog opens with a tab for each variant.
2. Compare the variants. Each tab shows your mockup beside the **DMS blocks** pane, which draws the real blocks in your dashboard's theme.
3. Close the dialog and answer the assistant's question in the chat.

A preview changes nothing in the project. A variant with a **Custom sketch** badge proposes a component the DMS has no block for, drawn as static HTML and CSS. After a reload, the dialog can no longer show an attached mockup image, so attach it again to compare. [Preview constraints](/docs/modules/assistant/preview-constraints) lists the limits of previews and sketches.
