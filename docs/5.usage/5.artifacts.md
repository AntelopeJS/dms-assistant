---
title: Artifacts
description: Keep the assistant's reports, tables and charts, and open them again.
mode: how-to
---

An artifact is a view the assistant saves from its results: a report, a table or a chart. The assistant hands the dashboard the data alone, a title and blocks (headings, paragraphs, lists, KPIs, tables and charts), and the dashboard draws them. Nothing the assistant writes is read as HTML, so an artifact shows every text as it was written. Each artifact belongs to the conversation that produced it and is visible to that conversation's owner.

## Ask for an artifact

Ask the assistant for a report, a comparison, a table or a chart of figures, or to keep what it found. It saves the artifact and ends its reply with a link that opens it. You can also open it later from **Artifacts** in the **Assistant** menu, at `/modules/ai/artifacts?artifact=<id>`.

The chat lists the call as **Create artifact** with the artifact's title, and the Activity page logs it like any other tool.

## Find an artifact

**Artifacts** lists every artifact of your conversations, newest first, with its kind, when it was saved and who asked. **Search artifacts** matches titles and summaries; **All**, **Report**, **Table** and **Chart** narrow the list to a kind. Click an artifact to open it beside the list; the URL keeps it, so a reload or a shared link opens it again.

## Read an artifact

The artifact opens on its kind, when it was saved, which agent wrote it and who asked, then its title and summary. Its blocks follow:

- **KPIs**: a label, a figure and an optional detail, in the tone the assistant chose.
- **Tables**: one cell per column; a figure reads in your regional format.
- **Charts**: bars or lines, drawn in the theme's chart colours, with a legend of the series.

**Open conversation** opens the chat the artifact came from.

## Delete an artifact

1. Open the artifact, then click **Delete artifact**.
2. Read the dialog, **Delete this artifact?**, which names it.
3. Click **Delete**.

The artifact leaves the list for good; the conversation it came from is kept. Only the owner of that conversation sees the artifact, so only they can delete it.

## If an artifact can't be shown

When a stored artifact is not in a shape the dashboard draws, the page says **This artifact can't be shown**. Ask the assistant to save it again.
