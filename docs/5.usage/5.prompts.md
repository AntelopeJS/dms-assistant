A saved prompt is a request you give a name to, so you can run it again without typing it. Saved prompts are yours: another member of the project sees neither your list nor your prompts. They live with the assistant's other records, in the project's `node_modules/.cache/dms-assistant/` folder.

## Save a prompt

From the chat:

1. Type the request in the composer.
2. Click the bookmark button next to the attachment button, **Save as prompt**.
3. Give it a name, up to 80 characters, then click **Save**. The request stays in the composer, so you can still send it.

From the **Prompts** page, `/modules/ai/prompts`:

1. Click **New prompt**.
2. Fill in **Name** and **Prompt**, then click **Save**.

Two of your prompts cannot share a name. The name's case and spacing do not count: `Weekly revenue` and `weekly  revenue` are one name. A prompt takes up to 20,000 characters.

## Run a prompt again

- On the **Prompts** page, click **Run** on the prompt. The assistant opens on a new conversation and sends the prompt at once, with the conversation's default scope and approval mode.
- In a new chat, the empty conversation lists your saved prompts under the page's starters. Click one to put it in the composer, then press `Enter` to send it. **Manage** opens the Prompts page.

A run from a saved prompt is a conversation like any other: it shows in the history, its tool calls land on the [Activity](/docs/modules/assistant/usage/activity) page, and its change sets on the [Changes](/docs/modules/assistant/usage/changes) page.

## Edit or delete a prompt

On the **Prompts** page, each prompt has **Edit** and **Delete**. **Delete** asks first. Deleting a prompt removes it from your list; the conversations it started stay.

The search box filters your prompts on their names and texts.
